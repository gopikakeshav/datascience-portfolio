About

Projects

Customer Churn Analysis

Using pandas to identify the factors that led to cancellation of customer subscriptions at a music store. Data driven results showed that customers who signed up for discounts were more likely to cancel and that the pop music enthusiasts were loyal customers of the music service.

MLB Player Analysis

Employing MySQL to understand the evolving changes in MLB player stats and team composition. Using Window functions, CTEs and subqueries to find that University of Texas produced the most players, more than 25 players started and ended their careers in the same team.

Sales Analysis

Using Tableau to analyse the company’s business performance over 3 years, detailing the sales, profit and order quantities by different geographical locations. With interactive filters and YoY comparison it is easier to spot the growth and decline in sales and thereby take corrective action.
